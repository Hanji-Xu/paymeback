Method

What the page does with your bills, and why you can trust the numbers.

How the simplifying works

Every bill turns into debts: each person who shared it owes the person who paid. A trip with a few bills can leave a tangle of small debts. Pay Me Back untangles them with five moves, one step at a time. No move changes what anyone owes or gets in total.

Add bills
Each bill is drawn as arrows from the people who shared it to the people who paid.
Net pairs
If Ana owes Ben 30 and Ben owes Ana 10, Ana owes Ben 20.
Cancel loops
If Ana owes Ben, Ben owes Cy and Cy owes Ana, the same amount comes off all three.
Skip middlemen
If Ana owes Ben and Ben owes Cy, Ana pays Cy directly.
Swap payees
If Ana and Ben both owe Cy and Dee, who pays whom is swapped around so that one payment drops out.

FEWEST PAYMENTS lets anyone pay anyone. ONLY EXISTING DEBTS keeps every payment between two people who already owed each other.

What accurate means here

Whole cents
Every amount is a whole number of cents, or of yen for currencies without cents. There are no fractions to drift.
Fair rounding
When a split does not divide evenly, the spare cents go to the shares that were cut the most. The parts always add up to the bill.
Checked at every step
After each step the page checks that every person still owes or gets exactly what they did before, and says so under the graph.
Dated rates
A bill in another currency shows its rate, the date of that rate and where it came from. Rates stay as they are until someone presses UPDATE RATES, so everyone with the link sees the same numbers.
No guessing
A line that can be read two ways is marked FIX, with what to change. It is never added as a guess.

Where your data goes

Your groups are saved in this browser. To share one, press SHARE: the whole group is packed into the link itself. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts.

If someone sends you a link to a group you already have and theirs is different, you choose which version to keep. Yours is never replaced without asking.

The page loads no fonts, no pictures and no trackers. The only outside request is for exchange rates, at most once a day. Rates by Exchange Rate API.
